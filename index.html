(01) Profile

Defence and offence, on purpose.

I work across both sides of the fence. By day I research SaaS security posture: reading vendor APIs, finding the settings that quietly leave the door open, and writing deterministic checks that catch them, mapped to CIS and SOC 2. The rest of the time I think like the person trying to get in.

Most of the job is translation. A finding is only useful once it becomes a fix someone can ship, so I spend as much time validating real risk as I do hunting for it.

Off the clock I trek and deliberately disconnect from screens. Mountains teach the same lesson as incident reviews: preparation matters, and shortcuts are rarely efficient.
